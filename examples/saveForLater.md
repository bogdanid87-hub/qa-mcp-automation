"Save for Later" Feature

Goal: Reduce cart abandonment and ease decision fatigue by allowing users to move items out of their active cart without deleting them.

1. Context & Objective
The Problem: Users often use the shopping cart as a "wishlist" and abandon their session when overwhelmed by high cart totals.
    Success Metrics:Decrease cart abandonment rate by 10%.
    Achieve 20% adoption of the "Save for Later" feature among active users.
    Increase overall 30-day cart conversion rates by 5%.

2. User Story
As an indecisive shopper, I want to move items to a "Save for Later" list, so that my cart is decluttered while keeping the items easily accessible for future purchase.

3. Functional Requirements
UI Placement: Display a "Save for Later" button next to the "Remove" button for each item in the cart.
The List: Create a dedicated "Saved for Later" section directly below the active shopping cart summary.
Move Back: Include a "Move to Cart" button within the Saved section to quickly restore items.
Pricing Updates: Apply dynamic updates to saved items if their price drops or stock runs low, triggering a visual badge.

4. Out of Scope for MVP
Automated 48-hour email or push notification reminders for saved items.
Categorizing saved items into folders (this will be evaluated for V2).
